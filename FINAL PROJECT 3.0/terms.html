SmartSave is a free, offline-first tool — no account registration is required and no data is transmitted to any server.

You are welcome to use SmartSave for personal budget tracking and financial management. When using this application, you agree to:

🔒 3. Your Data & Privacy

SmartSave takes your privacy seriously. Here is exactly how your data is handled:

  • All data is stored locally on your own browser using localStorage. It never leaves your device.
  • No accounts, no sign-ups. We do not collect your name, email, or any personal information.
  • No third-party tracking. SmartSave does not use analytics tools, cookies, or advertising trackers.
  • No server communication. This is a fully static web application — there is no backend or database.
  • You can delete all stored data at any time by clicking "Reset All History" on the History page, or by clearing your browser's site data.
