✦ Student Finance Guide

Financial Insights

Smart tips and practical guides to help you stretch every peso of your allowance.

📌 Budgeting Basics

📐The 50/30/20 Rule

Divide your weekly allowance: 50% for needs (food, fare, school supplies), 30% for wants (snacks, entertainment), and 20% for savings. Even saving ₱20/day adds up to ₱600 a month!

📓Track Every Peso

The moment you receive your allowance, log it in the Tracker. Students who record expenses — even small ones like ₱10 candy — develop stronger spending awareness within 2 weeks.

🎯Set a Weekly Spending Limit

Before the week starts, decide on a max spending amount. When you hit 80% of it, pause and ask: "Is what I'm about to buy a need or a want?"

💡 Smart Saving Habits

💷Pay Yourself First

The moment you receive your allowance, immediately set aside your savings amount before spending anything. Even ₱20–₱50 a day builds a habit. Savings shouldn't be what's left — they should come first.

☕The ₱50 Latte Effect

That ₱50 coffee or milk tea every school day costs roughly ₱1,000/month and ₱10,000/school year. You don't have to quit — just track it and decide if it's worth it.

🏠The Envelope Method

Divide your weekly allowance into labeled envelopes: Food, Transport, School, Savings. When an envelope is empty — stop spending in that category.

📁 Common Student Expense Categories

🍱Food & Drinks

Often the biggest variable expense. Try bringing packed lunch 2–3 days a week. Even just ₱50 saved per day = ₱1,000/month back in your pocket.

🚌Transportation

Track jeepney, bus, and tricycle rides. If you take the same route daily, calculate the monthly cost. Carpooling with classmates can cut this in half.

📚School Supplies

Buy in bulk at the start of the semester. Share printing costs with groupmates. Use your school library instead of buying textbooks when possible.

⚠️ Warning Signs to Watch

🔴Running Out Before the Week Ends

If you consistently run out of allowance by Wednesday or Thursday, review your history and find the category that's eating most of your budget.

🛍Impulse Buying

Before any unplanned purchase, wait 24 hours. If you still want it the next day and it fits your budget — buy it guilt-free. Most impulse urges fade within hours.

📱Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

Streaming services and mobile games silently drain your budget. List every active subscription and cancel ones you rarely use. ₱99/month × 5 apps = ₱495 gone quietly.
